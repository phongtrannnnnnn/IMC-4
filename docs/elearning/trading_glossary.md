# Trading Glossary

> Source: https://imc-prosperity.notion.site/trading-glossary

## Key Trading Terms

### Order Book
The order book is a list of all outstanding buy and sell orders for a particular product. It shows the price and quantity at each price level.

### Bid (Buy Order)
A bid is an order to buy a product at a specified price. The **best bid** is the highest-priced buy order in the order book.

### Ask (Sell Order)
An ask is an order to sell a product at a specified price. The **best ask** is the lowest-priced sell order in the order book.

### Spread
The difference between the best ask and the best bid. A tight spread indicates a liquid market.

```
Spread = Best Ask − Best Bid
```

### Mid Price
The average of the best bid and best ask prices.

```
Mid Price = (Best Bid + Best Ask) / 2
```

### Market Making
A strategy where a trader continuously places both buy and sell orders, profiting from the spread between them. Market makers provide liquidity to the market.

### Taking Liquidity
Placing an order that immediately matches with an existing order in the book. For example, placing a buy order at or above the best ask price.

### Providing Liquidity
Placing an order that does not immediately match — it sits in the order book waiting for a counterparty.

### Position
The net amount of a product that you hold. A positive position means you are "long" (you hold the product), a negative position means you are "short" (you owe the product).

### Position Limit
The maximum number of units of a product you can hold (long or short) at any point in time. Orders that would exceed the limit are rejected entirely.

### P&L (Profit and Loss)
The total profit or loss from your trading activity, measured in XIRECs.

```
P&L = Realized P&L + Unrealized P&L

Realized P&L = Sum of completed trade profits
Unrealized P&L = Current Position × (Current Mid Price − Average Entry Price)
```

### Fair Value
The theoretical "true" price of a product. Trading strategies aim to estimate fair value and profit from deviations.

### Arbitrage
Taking advantage of price differences between related products or markets to earn risk-free profit.

### Volatility
A measure of how much a product's price fluctuates over time. Higher volatility means larger price swings.

### Conversion
In Prosperity, some products can be converted between different forms, subject to fees (transport, tariffs). This enables cross-product arbitrage.

### XIREC
The currency used in Prosperity 4. All P&L is measured in XIRECs.

### Clearing Price
In an auction, the single price at which all qualifying orders execute. Selected to maximize total traded volume, with ties broken by the higher price.
