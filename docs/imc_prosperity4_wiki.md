## What is Prosperity?

Prosperity 4 is IMC's global online trading challenge, designed for university students who want to get familiar with algorithmic trading and financial markets. A unique simulation game developed by a team of IMC traders, quantitative researchers, and software engineers to provide an accessible, life-like experience of what it takes to be a trader.

This year, Prosperity 4 challenges you to explore new frontiers as you and your crew set course for outer space. Along the way, you'll encounter the most remarkable tradable goods and navigate fascinating foreign markets. It will take skill, knowledge, and courage to overcome the challenges you'll face and turn risk into reward, earning as many **XIRECs** (Prosperity currency) as possible.

The challenge runs for **16 days** in total, divided into **two active phases of six days each**, with a **four-day intermission** in between. After signing up, you will have time to explore the basic controls and dynamics of the challenge during the tutorial round. This tutorial round will last until April 14, when your first official mission starts.

Prove your worth on your first mission and you'll be granted access to the second, and final phase of the competition. At the end of the final round, the team that has generated the most profit of all teams will be victorious and be crowned **IMC Trading Talent of 2026**.

## Who is IMC?

IMC is a leading global market maker with offices in Amsterdam, Chicago, and Sydney. Founded in 1989, IMC uses proprietary technology and advanced algorithms to trade in financial markets worldwide.

IMC provides liquidity across multiple asset classes — including equities, bonds, commodities, and derivatives — and is one of the largest options market makers in the world.

IMC created Prosperity to give students and aspiring traders a hands-on experience of what trading at a market-making firm feels like:
- Market making and taking liquidity
- Managing risk and position limits
- Reacting to market events and data
- Building and optimizing trading algorithms

## Game Mechanics Overview

### Rounds
Prosperity 4 consists of multiple trading rounds, each introducing new tradable goods and gameplay elements. Each round features both algorithmic and manual trading components.

### Algorithmic Trading
Our market simulators are running 24/7. Each round has a specific time window during which your trades will be recorded for the official leaderboard. During this window, you need to ensure your algorithm is uploaded and running.

### Manual Trading
Next to algorithmic trading, each round also includes a manual trading challenge. These challenges vary in nature and will test your trading intuition and decision-making skills. Manual trade results represent a significant portion of your total score, so don't overlook them!

### Dashboard

#### Algorithmic Trading Submissions
Upload your Python algorithm through the **XIREN Uplink** on your Mission Control dashboard. Your algorithm will then be run against the market simulator. You can upload updated versions at any time during the round window. The last version uploaded before the round ends will be your official submission.

#### Manual Trading Submissions
Submit through the **Manual Challenge Overview** window, accessed by clicking the "Challenge Details" button under the Algorithmic Challenge.

#### Mission Control
You and your team can (re)submit strategies as many times as you please, but only the **last submitted trade** will be processed and executed at the end of the round.

### A.R.I.A. Uplinks
**Autonomous Relay Interface Assistant (A.R.I.A.)** Uplinks are your main source of information. A.R.I.A. will guide you through all essential information for your Algorithmic and Manual trading activities. A new Uplink will be available at the beginning of every round.

### Outpost View
The Outpost View provides a clear overview of your outpost and all the structures your team has unlocked by generating XIRECs profit. The more profit you make, the bigger your outpost will grow.

### Leaderboard
The global leaderboard is divided into multiple tabs:
- **Official Leaderboard**: Includes profit for every round (manual + algorithmic)
- **Final Winner Selection Board**: Top teams that qualify for the final determination at end of May

### Crew Honors
Teams that went above and beyond may be rewarded with cosmetic **Crew Honors** — symbols of hard work and dedication.

### On-Board Advisors
- Choose **one out of three** Advisors during the Tutorial Round
- You may switch freely during the Tutorial Round
- Selection is **locked** once the Tutorial Round ends
- From Round 1 onwards, your advisor provides insights and marketplace guidance
- **Choose carefully** — this is permanent for the entire Prosperity challenge

## Writing an Algorithm in Python

### General Rules
- Your algorithm must be written in **Python 3.10**
- Your submission must be a **single Python file** named `trader.py`
- Your file must contain a class named `Trader`
- The `Trader` class must have a `run` method that takes a `TradingState` object as an argument

### Technical Specification

The technical implementation is based on an **AWS Lambda function**. Because Lambda is stateless, AWS cannot guarantee that any class or global variables will be preserved between calls. The `run` method is the only entry point guaranteed to be called for every iteration.

#### The `run()` Method
- **Input:** `TradingState` object containing all market data for the current timestamp
- **Output:** A tuple of three items:
  1. `result`: `Dict[Symbol, List[Order]]` — orders to place
  2. `conversions`: `int` — conversion orders
  3. `traderData`: `str` — state to persist to next iteration

### Example Implementation

```python
from datamodel import OrderDepth, UserId, TradingState, Order
from typing import List
import string

class Trader:
    def run(self, state: TradingState):
        print("traderData: " + state.traderData)
        print("Observations: " + str(state.observations))

        result = {}
        for product in state.order_depths:
            order_depth: OrderDepth = state.order_depths[product]
            orders: List[Order] = []
            acceptable_price = 10

            if len(order_depth.sell_orders) != 0:
                best_ask, best_ask_amount = list(order_depth.sell_orders.items())[0]
                if int(best_ask) < acceptable_price:
                    orders.append(Order(product, best_ask, -best_ask_amount))

            if len(order_depth.buy_orders) != 0:
                best_bid, best_bid_amount = list(order_depth.buy_orders.items())[0]
                if int(best_bid) > acceptable_price:
                    orders.append(Order(product, best_bid, -best_bid_amount))

            result[product] = orders

        traderData = ""
        conversions = 1
        return result, conversions, traderData
```

### Supported Libraries

| Library | Version |
|---------|---------|
| `pandas` | 1.5.3 |
| `numpy` | 1.24.2 |
| `statistics` | stdlib |
| `math` | stdlib |
| `typing` | stdlib |
| `jsonpickle` | 3.0.1 |

> **No other libraries are supported. Any attempt to access external data or APIs will result in disqualification.**

### Technical Constraints
- **Time Limit:** 900ms per `run()` call
- **Position Limits:** Per-product; orders exceeding the limit are rejected entirely
- **State Persistence:** Use the `traderData` string (serialize with `json.dumps()` or `jsonpickle.encode()`)

### State Persistence Pattern
```python
import json

class Trader:
    def run(self, state: TradingState):
        if state.traderData:
            my_state = json.loads(state.traderData)
        else:
            my_state = {"prices": [], "trades": 0}

        # ... trading logic ...

        traderData = json.dumps(my_state)
        return result, conversions, traderData
```

- **External data:** Only data provided in `TradingState` may be used

## FAQ

### Getting Started

**Who can participate and/or win?**
While designed for university students, the platform is open to anyone. However, Top 25 eligibility requires proof of university enrollment, age 18+, and teams of 1-5 members.

**How do I write a trading program?**
Upload a Python file containing a `Trader` class. See [Writing an Algorithm in Python](#writing-an-algorithm-in-python).

**What is an On-Board Advisor?**
Choose one of three advisors during the Tutorial Round. Each has different strengths and provides unique insights. Once chosen, the selection is permanent.

### Technical

**What version of Python is used?** Python 3.10.

**What libraries can I use?** `pandas`, `NumPy`, `statistics`, `math`, `typing`, and `jsonpickle`. No others.

**Can I use external data?** No — only data from the `TradingState` object. External data/APIs = disqualification.

### Ranking and Prizes

**How is my total score calculated?**
Algorithmic trading profit + manual trading results combined. Each round contributes to the global leaderboard.

**What happens if there's a tie?**
The team that achieved their final profit first is ranked higher.

### Challenge Submissions & Logistics

**Will the leaderboard reset for Phase 2?**
**Yes.** Rounds 1 & 2 are qualifiers. Teams must secure **200,000 XIRECs** to lock in their spot for Phase 2 (Rounds 3, 4, 5).

**Does a new round open immediately after the previous?**
There is a ~3 hour calculation period between rounds.

**Do Manual Challenges affect Algorithmic PnL?**
No, they are independent. Manual challenges are a way to earn extra profit.

**Maximum team size?** 5 people.

**Can I leave a team?** Yes, through Team Settings, as long as team formation is not yet locked.
